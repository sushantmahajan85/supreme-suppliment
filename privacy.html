Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Supreme Nutrition Enterprises handles personal information when you visit supremenutrition.in, place an order, contact us, or otherwise interact with us.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Contact and order details such as your name, phone number, email address, billing or delivery address, PIN code, products ordered and order history.
  • Payment status and transaction references from the payment provider. We do not intend to collect or store your full card number, CVV or online banking credentials.
  • Information you provide in messages, customer support requests, return requests or grievance submissions.
  • Basic technical information generated when you visit the website, such as device, browser, approximate location, pages visited and website activity, where collected by the website or its service providers.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Accept, process, deliver and support orders, including cash on delivery orders.
  • Send order, payment, delivery, cancellation and refund updates.
  • Respond to questions, complaints and product-safety reports.
  • Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, security incidents and misuse.
  • Maintain business and tax records and meet legal obligations.
  • Improve website performance and understand how visitors use the site, where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Send promotional messages only where applicable consent or other legal requirements are met. You can opt out of promotional communications using the method provided in the message or by contacting us.

When information may be shared

We may share only information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose with:

  • Payment service providers, to process prepaid transactions.
  • Delivery and fulfilment providers, to deliver orders and handle delivery issues.
  • Website hosting, communications, security and analytics providers used to operate the website.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement authorities or other persons where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to fulfil the purposes described above, maintain required business records, resolve disputes and comply with law. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, though no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Some information may need to be retained to complete an order, meet legal duties, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Cookies and similar tools

This website does not use advertising cookies, and it does not currently run an analytics tool that tracks you across other sites. The checkout may keep a short-lived item in your browser’s session storage so the same payment attempt is not submitted twice. That item stays on your device and is cleared when the session ends or the payment is finished.

Prepaid payment is completed on Razorpay’s page. WhatsApp orders open WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy practices. If we later enable analytics or advertising tools, this section will name them and explain how to control them.

Children

The website and products are not directed to children. Do not submit a child’s personal information through the website.

Changes

We may update this Policy when our practices or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
