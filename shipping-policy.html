Shipping policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains where we deliver, how long it takes and what happens if a delivery goes wrong, for orders placed with Supreme Nutrition Enterprises through this website or on WhatsApp.

  • Free

    Shipping on every order, anywhere in India.

  • 48 hours

    To dispatch your order after payment.

  • 2–7 days

    Typical delivery time after dispatch.

1. Where we deliver

We ship to addresses within India only. If our courier partner cannot deliver to your PIN code, we will contact you before dispatch and refund the full amount you paid.

2. Shipping charges

Shipping is free on every order. The price you see at checkout includes all taxes, and no delivery charge is added.

3. When your order is dispatched

We pack and dispatch orders within 48 hours of receiving payment. We do not dispatch on Sundays or public holidays, so orders placed on those days leave on the next working day.

4. Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes:

  • Metro cities: 2–4 working days;
  • Other PIN codes: 4–7 working days;
  • Remote areas such as parts of the North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and the islands: a few days longer.

These times are estimates, not guarantees. Weather, festivals, strikes or courier delays can slow a delivery down. If your order is taking longer than expected, message us and we will chase the courier for you.

5. Tracking your order

Once your order is dispatched, we send the courier name and tracking details to the WhatsApp number or email address you gave at checkout. If you have not heard from us within 48 hours of paying, please get in touch.

6. Your address and phone number

Please check your delivery address, PIN code and phone number before you pay, because the courier may call you to complete the delivery. If you need to change the address, tell us before the order is dispatched. Once a parcel has left our facility we may not be able to redirect it.

7. Failed deliveries and returned parcels

The courier usually makes more than one attempt to deliver. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was incorrect, the phone was unreachable or the delivery was refused, we will contact you and either send it again or refund the order. If the delivery failed for one of those reasons, we may charge the cost of sending it a second time.

8. Damaged or tampered parcels

Check the outer box when it arrives. If it looks opened or damaged, you can refuse the parcel, or accept it and record an unboxing video. Report any damaged, missing or incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery, as explained in our refund & return policy.

9. How to reach us

For any delivery question, message us with your order reference.
