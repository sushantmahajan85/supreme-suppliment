Refund & return policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how cancellations, returns, replacements and refunds work for orders placed with Supreme Nutrition Enterprises through this website or on WhatsApp.

  • Before dispatch

    Cancel for a full refund.

  • 48 hours

    To report a damaged, missing or wrong item.

  • 10 days

    To return an unopened, sealed product.

1. Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order at any time before it leaves our facility. Message us on WhatsApp or email us with your order reference, and we will refund the full amount you paid. Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled, but you may still be able to return it under section 3.

2. Damaged, missing or incorrect items

If your kit arrives damaged, with a product missing, or with the wrong product, tell us within 48 hours of delivery. Please send:

  • your order reference;
  • clear photos of the outer box, the shipping label and the products;
  • an unboxing video, if you recorded one — it helps us resolve the claim fastest.

Once we have checked the claim, we will send a replacement or refund the affected item at no extra cost to you. Claims raised more than 48 hours after delivery may not be accepted, because we can no longer confirm what happened in transit.

3. Returns

You can request a return within 10 days of delivery if the product is:

  • unused, with the safety seal intact;
  • in its original packaging, including the box, diet guide and progress tracker.

For safety and hygiene reasons we cannot accept the return of any product whose seal has been broken or that has been partly used, unless it was damaged or incorrect on arrival (section 2). Contact us before sending anything back; we will confirm whether the return is approved and share the return instructions.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Online (Razorpay) payments are refunded to the original payment method.
  • Orders paid any other way are refunded by bank transfer or UPI to an account you share with us.
  • We start the refund once a cancellation is confirmed or a returned product has been received and checked. Your bank or payment provider usually takes 5–7 business days to show the money in your account.

5. Misuse and tampering

We may refuse a refund or replacement where a product shows signs of tampering, where a claim appears to be false, or where this policy is being repeatedly misused. In those cases we will explain our decision to you.

6. How to reach us

Every request starts with a message that includes your order reference. A person reads every message.

  • Postal address: Supreme Nutrition Enterprises, Gole Market, 5A, Model Town, Panipat, Haryana 132103
